AI is like a paid multiplier. Whatever you put in, gets scaled and amplified. 

I'm a product manager with a background in Design Systems and accessibility. When it comes to accessibility, there are questions everywhere. For example: "Can AI make accessible websites?", "Can AI make our website accessible?" and "Can AI do accessibility testing?" Systems that hallucinate don't mix well with regulations and absolute requirements. The answer is usually: no.

AI is "[garbage in, garbage out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garbage_in,_garbage_out)". The models that we use are fed with large quantities of (I'm sorry) [crappy code](https://webaim.org/projects/million/) when it comes to accessibility. And the output reflects that. 

To make things worse, often we don't even know which specific piece of garbage is behind the suggestions we get from models. I have had models mention laws that don't exist, and then stubbornly give me made-up links to those non-existing laws. Pure compliance-horror!

And lots of people lean on these systems to produce code. Models get asked to contribute to code, and they get the trust to produce something but they work with input we know nothing about. If these weren't models or agents but people,  at least you could introduce education on topics.

The case for Design Systems is different than for accessibility. A Design System is like education for agents! 

Introduce an agent to a Design System, and you know exactly what goes in. You know that what it suggests is built up from compliant patterns and components. You can provide the ultimate guardrails. 

Anybody can ask a model to build something and it'll happily go off and produce code. But without the right guidance, it can go in the wrong direction, use the wrong input and before you know it, you've lost all your productivity being busy correcting the output. Unguided effort and costs result in worthless AI slop.

Give a model the right direction, and it will follow your UX principles, your branding guidelines, your regulatory compliance, your best practices in coding and everything else you put into your Design System, accessibility for example! 

AI is like a paid multiplier. Whatever you put in, gets scaled and amplified. It is up to us to decide what we put in. By default it is random internet-content. We can educate our agents, make the most of investments, and choose to leverage a high quality Design System instead.

![Lego set 76443: Hagrid & Harry's Motorcycle Ride. A great example of how bad something can look, even with the right pieces.](/assets/media/hagrid-harry.webp)

Disclaimer: you still need people in the loop. A great system as a foundation does not guarantee great output. (Like Lego Harry and Hagrid here.)
However, terrible building blocks make terrible results pretty much a certainty.
