TL;DR: I've got a bad balance but I want to run 5k. How can I make it to the end despite this?

## So yeah, how did we get here?
I've had several attempts at [Couch to 5k (c25k)](https://c25k.com/), and they mostly stranded for different reasons. The (or a?) thing is I have an acquired brain injury from 3 surgeries, and that makes my balance... below average. It's not that I have a wheelchair, crutches or special footwear. I'm just more clumsy. I stumble more. And I don't always walk as straight as I'd like. Especially when tired.
History:

- I've done a c25k where my knees started to hurt, I got a run analysis, and got some "real" shoes instead of generic sports shoes. I overpronated. And I got some Brooks Adrenaline Gts. It's been a while, I think it was 16. Replaced them since with 23. And probably could use new ones.
- I've done a c25k before I went into surgery, and couldn't continue.
- I've done a c25k and I broke my arm, and had to recover from that first.
- I've done a c25k where one day my foot hurt enormously shortly after a run. I had to recover from that. Suspected Plantar fasciitis.
- I've done a c25k where I developed a massive hump under my knee. Suspected bursitis.

And I know from the past few times that with time my shins get painful and sensitive. Both while a singular run progresses, but also when I get further into the c25k schedule. I don't know if that's shin splints or something else, but I do know that that's a bigger limiting factor than my stamina. (Even though I can be absolutely and extremely tired after a run. I like to push myself and that's fine.)

## What I have done already
- I got better shoes (but my maybe I'm up for a new pair and/or analysis)
- I once ran with a ±130bpm playlist. These days I'm at around 170bpm and comparative cadence. Hopefully this reduces stress on joints and muscles. It also feels really good. More fluid.
- I have a treadmill at my disposal
- I have been exercising 3 times a week, including lunges and squats in preparation of this edition
- The road here got paved. Since my first attempts, we moved, and we've got asfalt here but not perfectly level all the way.

## Now what?
I'm in doubt what thing or things I should do.
- Should I do C25K, but every week twice before going to the next?
- Should it be even slower? Triple? Quad?
- Should I take more rest in between, for example, instead of 3 runs in week, 4 in 2 weeks?
- Should I do a clinic, to learn about the best form (and hope I can keep it up)?
- Should I get newer shoes than the '23 I have now?
- Should I incorporate the treadmill somehow?
- A combination?
- Something else entirely?

How would you approach building towards a 5K when your joints and muscles are the biggest challenge?
